RAGkit — mobile & tablet
Rules, then the screens. Tablet ≥768px keeps the desktop architecture — persistent sidebar, same thread; nothing to redesign. Phone <768px makes four moves: the sidebar becomes a drawer; every evidence card becomes a bottom sheet (the desktop card was already bottom-anchored, so it's the same object); the scope picker becomes a bottom sheet off the header pill; the composer pins to the bottom with its state line above it, still speaking only on deviation. Citation chips keep their size in text but carry a 44×44 tap target via padding-box. Evidence colors, tags, and copy are unchanged — provenance doesn't simplify on small screens, it just re-stacks.
